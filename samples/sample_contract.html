SERVICE AGREEMENT

This Service Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of January 1, 2025 ("Effective Date"), between Alpha Corporation, a company incorporated under the laws of Vietnam ("Service Provider"), and Beta Ltd., a company incorporated under the laws of Vietnam ("Client").

Chapter I — General Provisions

Article 1 — Definitions

For the purposes of this Agreement, the following terms shall have the meanings set forth below:

  • "Services" means the software development and consulting services described in Annex A.
  • "Deliverables" means any work product, report, or output produced by the Service Provider.
  • "Confidential Information" means any non-public information disclosed by either Party.

Article 2 — Scope of Services

The Service Provider agrees to provide the Services set out in Annex A attached hereto, subject to the terms and conditions of this Agreement. The Client shall cooperate with the Service Provider and provide timely access to information required for performance.

Chapter II — Payment Terms

Article 3 — Fees and Payment

The Client shall pay the Service Provider the fees set forth in the following schedule:

Milestone Description Amount (USD) Due Date
Milestone 1 Project Kickoff and Requirements 10,000 Upon signing
Milestone 2 Prototype Delivery 15,000 March 31, 2025
Milestone 3 Final Delivery and Acceptance 25,000 June 30, 2025

Article 4 — Late Payment

If the Client fails to pay any amount by the due date, the Service Provider may charge interest at a rate of 1.5% per month on the outstanding balance, compounded monthly, from the date payment was due until payment is received in full.

Chapter III — Confidentiality and IP

Article 5 — Confidentiality Obligations

(1) Each Party shall maintain the confidentiality of the other Party's Confidential Information and shall not disclose it to any third party without prior written consent.

(2) The obligations of confidentiality shall not apply to information that:

  • (a) is or becomes publicly available through no fault of the receiving Party;
  • (b) was already known to the receiving Party prior to disclosure;
  • (c) is required to be disclosed by applicable law or court order.

Article 6 — Intellectual Property

All Deliverables produced under this Agreement shall, upon full payment of all fees, become the sole and exclusive property of the Client. The Service Provider retains no rights in the Deliverables except as expressly provided herein.

Chapter IV — Term and Termination

Article 7 — Term

This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and shall continue in full force until completion of the Services, unless earlier terminated in accordance with Article 8.

Article 8 — Termination for Convenience

Either Party may terminate this Agreement for convenience upon thirty (30) days' prior written notice to the other Party. In such event, the Client shall pay the Service Provider for all Services performed up to the effective date of termination.

Article 9 — Termination for Cause

Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately upon written notice if the other Party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure such breach within fifteen (15) business days after receiving written notice of the breach.

Chapter V — Liability and Indemnification

Article 10 — Limitation of Liability

In no event shall either Party be liable to the other for any indirect, incidental, special, consequential, or punitive damages arising out of or related to this Agreement, even if such Party has been advised of the possibility of such damages.

Article 11 — Indemnification

Each Party shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless the other Party from and against any claims, liabilities, losses, damages, costs, and expenses (including reasonable legal fees) arising from such Party's breach of this Agreement or gross negligence or willful misconduct.

Chapter VI — General Provisions

Article 12 — Governing Law

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam, without regard to its conflict of law provisions.

Article 13 — Dispute Resolution

Any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall be submitted to the Vietnam International Arbitration Centre (VIAC) for final and binding arbitration in accordance with its rules. The seat of arbitration shall be Hanoi, Vietnam.

Article 14 — Entire Agreement

This Agreement, including all Annexes attached hereto, constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties with respect to the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior negotiations, representations, warranties, and agreements.
